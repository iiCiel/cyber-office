# Cyber Office playtest kit (M7)

Everything needed to run the M7 playtest with 2-3 people. Send each tester the
message below, watch (or have them screen-record) one session, then copy their
answers into the results table at the bottom.

## Message to send a tester

> Hi! I'm building a browser game where you spend a day as a junior security
> analyst at a fake insurance company. Could you play it for about 20 minutes
> and answer 8 quick questions after? No security background needed.
>
> 1. Open https://iiciel.github.io/cyber-office/ on a laptop or desktop (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).
> 2. Click **Monday** (or press 1) and play until the report card appears.
> 3. If you still have time, press **F** on the title screen and try one Case File (Uber 2022 is a good first one).
> 4. Please think out loud or jot down any moment you were confused, bored or stuck.
>
> Controls: click or arrow keys / WASD to walk, E (or Space) to interact, Esc to close a screen, P for the pause menu (it has a glossary of security terms), M to mute.

## What to watch for (if you can sit with them)

- First 2 minutes: do they find their desk and the inbox without help?
- Do they open the email "Explain" / header view on their own, or never notice it?
- SIEM: do they type a search, or get stuck staring at the console?
- Where do they pause for more than ~30 seconds? Note the exact screen.
- Do they read the debrief and report card, or click straight past it?
- Any bug, freeze, overlapping text or button that didn't respond.

## The 8 questions

1. How long did Monday take you (roughly)?
2. Was there a moment you didn't know what to do next? Where?
3. Which part was the most fun or interesting?
4. Which part felt like a chore, or too much text?
5. Did anything feel fake or wrong (emails, logs, coworkers)?
6. Did the report card's grade feel fair for how you played?
7. On a scale of 1-5, how likely would you be to play Tuesday?
8. Anything that broke (bug, freeze, typo)? A screenshot helps.
